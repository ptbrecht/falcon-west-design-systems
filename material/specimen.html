The one-sentence read

Slightly industrial,
not friendly-rounded.

White and near-white surfaces. Square cards and large containers (radius 0). Softly-rounded 4px controls. Rust-700 as the single action color. Navy as chrome — never a button fill. Raleway throughout. Apps and serial products only, not the marketing sites.

The rust rule
#D2793D is 3.23:1 on white — legal for the mark, icons, and rules. Illegal for any fill that carries a label. Text-bearing rust steps down to #a85f2e. White text on logo orange is wrong even when it looks most on-brand. On navy, #D2793D is banned entirely; use #e0a06e.
