# Personal Lines — writing guidelines
Site: falconwest.com (personal / private-client pages, Coverage Scene, personal Insights, newsletter teasers)

## Reader
Successful / accomplished homeowners and families — not “affluent” or “high net worth” on skim UI. One reader in the room (the homeowner wiring closing funds, the family with a pool and an ADU).

## Voice
- Warm **you / we**; complete sentences; softer verbs (help protect, cover, support).
- Since 1981 private-client tone. Not Clickable Coverage branding on modals.
- Chuck craft for Insights/newsletter: cold open, short sentences, earned first person, soft product late. Guide: [`chuck-style.md`](chuck-style.md)
- Emulate craft, not persona. Peter is not old: never old-man narrator, “listen up kids,” or age theater.

## Chubb-aligned personal language (HNW / successful homeowners)
- successful / accomplished > affluent / high net worth (especially on skim pins)
- Lead experience and protection, not coverage scare
- “how to best protect you” not “risks and exposures”
- “if something happens” not “if a loss occurs”
- Positive what-you-get; because-of-your-success needs may have changed
- Relatable home details (pool, ADU, roof, trees) over luxury-flex examples
- Never “attractive nuisance” scare language

## Coverage Scene (locked direction as of 2026-09-29)
- Solution-first skim; one sentence per hotspot preferred
- Vary sentence patterns; lead with them / what they need — not “We help” on every pin
- No Risk Factor → Solution two-step

## House rules
- House thesis: save insurance for catastrophes
- Soft CTA; educational first
- No em dashes on falconwest.com Insights
- Never invent quotes, client names, facts, or claim files
- Named Mike / Peter quotes only when real
- Newsletters: ruthlessly short teasers that drive to Insights

## Do not retread (unless new hook)
CA homeowners 2026, HNW program design, AI-vs-broker, water mitigation, photo packets, Academy welcome — unless extending with a new angle.
