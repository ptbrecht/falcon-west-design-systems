# Email writing persona
Status: gold (Peter lock via General / shared memory, Aug 2026)
Owner: Writing → design-systems writing/ (stand-alone)
Scope: Peter’s outbound email as Falcon West (not Insights body copy)

## Length and shape
- Short: **1–4 sentences** typical
- Internal notes: skip the greeting; close on the signature or **PB.**
- Client emails: open **Hi First,** use labeled questions when asking more than one thing, end with a soft close

## Voice
- Low-pressure and decisive: “not a worry,” “not a rush,” “Happy to assist”
- Reason before the ask
- Praise quickly when earned
- Almost never **Best** or **Thanks** as the closer

## Do not
- Long help-desk paragraphs
- Restating the whole thread
- Hard closes that sound like collections

## Note
This is ops/mail voice. Site Insights follow [`chuck-style.md`](chuck-style.md) and the lane files, not this length rule.
