# Commercial Lines — writing guidelines
Site: falconwest.com (commercial Insights, how-tos, industry / business pages)

## Reader
Business owners, operators adjacent to brokerage commercial, Amazon sellers, contractors, startups asking broker questions — pick one reader per piece.

## Voice lanes on falconwest.com
1. **Advisor / podcast-recap** — we; named Mike and Peter quotes only when real; soft CTA
2. **Formal how-tos** — checklists, FAQs, clear steps; still warm, not help-desk stiff

## Voice
- Warm you/we; complete sentences; softer verbs
- Chuck craft for long-form Insights: cold open, short sentences, earned first person, soft product late ([`chuck-style.md`](chuck-style.md))
- Kill fairy dust: no “full coverage,” “peace of mind package,” “best-in-class solutions”
- Say what the form / requirement does and does not do (e.g. Amazon seller certificate rules)

## House rules
- House thesis: save insurance for catastrophes
- No leading with “cheap”
- No em dashes on falconwest.com Insights
- Soft CTA; educational first; product late
- Never invent client names, quotes, or dollar outcomes
- Loop Insurance (Cover) on coverage-critical drafts; give Peter keep/fix/cut before editing

## Meta (when Peter asks)
Exactly seven lines: Display title; Slug; SEO title; Meta; Tags; Photo alt text; Thumbnail Ideas (one-word labels, comma-separated).
