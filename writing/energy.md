# Energy — writing guidelines
Site: falconwestenergy.com (Insights, industries accordion, bonding / O&G pages)

## Reader
Operators, contractors, landmen, consultants, midstream and well-service decision makers — one specific reader per piece (e.g. the operator buying Control of Well, the rigger reading an industries drop-down).

## Voice (Jul 2026 bonding / Insights lock)
- **Second person** with **first-person credibility**
- Specific regulators and dollar figures when true
- Specialist close
- Max **two** em dashes per piece
- No rust/orange in copy direction that implies brand color theater
- Surety pages: never name OneNexus or WellSecure
- Paste for Peter’s review before publish unless he says otherwise
- Use Insurance Knowledge / Insurance review for coverage accuracy when relevant

## Industries accordion / short site blocks
- Match existing drop-downs: ~55–75 words; “engaged us / we built” structure
- Highlight a real client shape when facts exist; otherwise clear placeholders ([CLIENT], [coverage placed], [result]) — never invent names or outcomes
- No vendor names unless Peter supplies them

## Insights craft
- Chuck craft still applies for nerve and clarity ([`chuck-style.md`](chuck-style.md)), filtered through Energy voice above
- Soft product late; specialist close

## Do not retread (unless new hook)
Bonding explainers, 45Q, captives, five red-flag cases, generic MSA 101, Control of Well / OEE — unless a new angle.

## White space (open topics)
Subsea / Gulf package, energy hauling, equipment rental, SWD, pollution, non-op as Insights, claims handling, 2026 carrier appetite, etc.
