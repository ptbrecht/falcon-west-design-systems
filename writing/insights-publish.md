## Seven-line meta pack (always, when meta is requested)
Deliver **exactly** these seven lines and nothing else as the meta block:

1. Display title
2. Slug
3. SEO title
4. Meta
5. Tags
6. Photo alt text
7. Thumbnail Ideas

**Thumbnail Ideas** = one-word labels, comma-separated (e.g. `Wellhead, Kick, Pressure`). Not multi-word scene descriptions. Do not reuse the previous article’s thumbnail set by accident.

## Chuck / craft publish checklist
From [`chuck-style.md`](chuck-style.md):
- [ ] First sentence could not appear in a carrier brochure
- [ ] At least one named receipt (person, place, number, statute, podcast, public figure)
- [ ] One clear reader (not “everyone”)
- [ ] Digression pays rent
- [ ] No fairy-dust phrases
- [ ] Product appears late or not at all
- [ ] Sentences mostly short; one punch line per section
- [ ] Accurate (Insurance review when coverage-critical)
- [ ] Em dashes within house limits (FW.com: none; Energy: max two)
- [ ] Closer is one line, then contact
- [ ] No age theater / old-man narrator

## Structure template
1. Cold open  
2. Bridge (why now; one hard receipt)  
3. Body (2–4 scenes)  
4. Pattern  
5. Do / don’t  
6. Soft next step (optional)  
7. One-line educational disclaimer  

## Newsletter
- Readers open and click quickly; they rarely read a long email
- Keep ruthlessly short as a **teaser** that drives to the full Insights piece
- Soft educational CTA only; do not finish the sell in the email
